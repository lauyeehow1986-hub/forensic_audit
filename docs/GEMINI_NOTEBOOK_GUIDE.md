# Forensic Research Auditor: Gemini Notebook Integration Guide

This guide details how to create, configure, and operate the **Forensic Research Auditor** Gemini Notebook (`https://gemini.google.com/notebook/6b8609aa-fa3d-4a82-a123-5ffe7b4518e1`). 

The Gemini Notebook serves as the **centralized metascience knowledge base and evidence synthesis engine** for all verified audit packets generated by this pipeline.

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

While the Google Antigravity `/boost` pipeline performs rigorous local computational verification (running `statcheck`, `scrutiny::grim_map`, `benford.analysis`, and trial registry diffing), the **Gemini Notebook** provides:

1. **Persistent Multimodal Audit Repository:** Centralizes audit reports across multiple trials, medical specialties, and therapeutic fields.
2. **Cross-Study Metascience Synthesis:** Surfaces systemic research practices across clinical fields (e.g., comparing silent outcome switching between device vs. pharmaceutical trials).
3. **Interactive Forensic Interrogation:** Allows researchers and peer reviewers to query evidence tables with zero hallucination via strict source grounding.
4. **Deep-Dive Audio Discussions:** Generates conversational audio reviews (Audio Overviews) that break down methodological flaws and Bayesian credibility scores for journal clubs and research teams.

## 2. Notebook Setup & Configuration

### A. Creating the Notebook
1. Navigate to [Gemini Notebooks](https://gemini.google.com/notebook/6b8609aa-fa3d-4a82-a123-5ffe7b4518e1).
2. Name the notebook:
   ```text
   Forensic Research Auditor
   ```

### B. Custom Instructions / System Persona
In the Notebook's settings or custom prompt instructions, paste the following system instruction to enforce adversarial peer review:

```text
You are the Forensic Research Auditor Metascience Swarm, operating in adversarial peer-review mode.
Your role is to analyze, synthesize, and interrogate forensic audit packets generated from clinical trials and scientific publications.

Core Operating Principles:
1. Strict Grounding: Rely exclusively on the uploaded audit packets, verbatim R stdout logs, and extracted registry data. Never assume, extrapolate, or hallucinate findings not grounded in the sources.
2. Non-Compensatory Evaluation: Methodological fatal flaws (unregistered subgroup spin, >2 GRIM errors, scatterplot data absence <5%) cannot be offset by journal prestige or citation counts.
3. Quantify Credibility: Always cite the Tier 2 Bayesian Posterior Credibility Score P(Credible) and detail individual Likelihood Ratio (LR) penalties.
4. Distinguish Biological vs. Behavioral Mechanisms: Explicitly dissect whether reported benefits stem from true therapeutic efficacy, unblinded co-intervention confounding (e.g., statin initiation), or detection bias.
```

## 3. Ingestion Workflow: Adding Audit Packets

Whenever an audit is completed in Antigravity, the verified packet is saved to:
```text
./audit_reports/audit_[STUDY_NAME]_[DOI_OR_ID].md
```

### Ingestion Steps:
1. Open the [Forensic Research Auditor Gemini Notebook](https://gemini.google.com/notebook/6b8609aa-fa3d-4a82-a123-5ffe7b4518e1).
2. Click **Add Sources** (`+` button in the left panel).
3. Select **Upload Files** and choose the generated markdown file(s) from `./audit_reports/`.
4. (Optional) You can also upload the official trial protocol PDF or ClinicalTrials.gov JSON export alongside the audit packet for dual-source cross-referencing.

## 4. Key Notebook Interrogation Prompts

Once audit packets are loaded into the notebook, use these standardized prompts to extract forensic insights:

### A. Cross-Study Outcome Switching Audit
> *"Across all currently loaded clinical trials, identify which studies downgraded their original pre-registered primary endpoint to a secondary endpoint or re-framed non-efficacy endpoints (e.g., safety/acceptability) to claim trial success. Provide a comparative Markdown table with verbatim registry quotes."*

### B. Subgroup Extrapolation & Boundary Leverage Analysis
> *"Review the Gate 3.5 Chart & Density Audits for all loaded trials. Which studies claimed therapeutic benefit in a severe disease subgroup where the actual data density in that range was below 5%? Explain the statistical leverage and show the Bayesian LR penalty applied."*

### C. Bayesian Credibility Ranking Matrix
> *"Generate a comparative Bayesian Credibility Scorecard for all loaded studies. Display: Study Name, Prior Odds, Individual Likelihood Ratios (LR1 through LR4), Posterior Odds, and Final Credibility Probability. Group studies into: Highly Credible (>75%), Plausible/Confounded (5–75%), and Spun/Fatally Compromised (<5%)."*

### D. Open-Label Performance & Co-Intervention Bias Check
> *"Analyze the Cochrane RoB 2 Domain 2 assessments across all studies. Identify trials where unblinded physicians initiated asymmetric co-medications (e.g., statins or antiplatelets) that could explain downstream event reductions without the intervention itself having direct biological efficacy."*

## 5. Generating Audio Overviews (Forensic Deep-Dives)

The Gemini Notebook supports generating 2-host conversational Audio Overviews from your sources:

1. Click **Audio Overview (Deep Dive)** in the notebook guide panel.
2. Gemini will synthesize the uploaded audit reports into an in-depth spoken discussion covering:
   - The original pre-registered trial hypotheses.
   - The verbatim R `statcheck` and `grim_map` results.
   - Why the published conclusions diverged from the empirical data.
   - The final Bayesian credibility determination.
3. Download or share the generated audio review for research seminars, editorial meetings, or lab discussions.

## 6. Maintaining Repository Hygiene

- **Keep Source Code Clean:** Do not commit study-specific audit reports or proprietary case studies into the public git repository.
- **Local Persistence:** Keep case study reports stored in your local `./audit_reports/` directory and upload them directly into your private Gemini Notebook.
